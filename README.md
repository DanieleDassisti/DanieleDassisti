### 🧠 The Rabbit Hole

Some people like clean abstractions. I like asking uncomfortable questions like *"Why is this memory leak happening at 3 AM?"* or *"Can this robot make a sensible decision without crashing into a wall?"*

My true obsession is **robotics, autonomous agents, and machine learning**. But here's the catch: I refuse to treat anything as a magic black box.
- To understand the **mind**, I went down the **Machine Learning** path: training neural networks, dealing with noisy sensor feeds, and optimizing spatial perception algorithms so robots can actually "see".
- To understand the **body**, I dove into **Embedded Systems**: because an autonomous drone or robot doesn't care about high-level elegance if your RTOS misses a deadline or your serial bus drops a frame.
- And when code meets physical reality, I enjoy hooking up **oscilloscopes and logic analyzers** to find out who's lying: my firmware, the hardware, or the physics textbook.

---

### 🕹️ What Keeps Me Busy

- 🤖 **Robotics, Drones & Exploration:** Sensor fusion (LiDAR, IMU, cameras), SLAM, path planning (A*, Dijkstra), and autonomous navigation powered by ROS / ROS 2 ecosystems.
- 🔬 **Applied Machine Learning & Vision:** Experimenting with PyTorch, numerical optimization, OpenCV pipelines, and keeping inference lean enough to run without setting edge devices on fire.
- ⚙️ **Bare-Metal & Embedded Wizardry:** RTOS tasks (FreeRTOS), Linux kernel internals, device trees, and memory management where every byte and millisecond counts.
- 🔬 **Lab Bench Detective Work:** Probing serial buses (UART, SPI, I2C, CAN), decoding signals with logic analyzers & scopes, JTAG/SWD debugging, and writing automated HIL tests.
- 🗄️ **Data & Tooling:** Taming relational data with SQL/PostgreSQL, blazing-fast state caching with Redis, scripting quick routines in Lua/Bash, and packing everything neatly into Docker.
